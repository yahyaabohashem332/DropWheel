## Short description

Drag a file, tap a key, drop it on a petal. Petal converts, compresses and fixes pictures, videos, music
and documents right on your PC: no uploads, no account, no waiting.

## Description

Petal puts a wheel under your cursor while you drag a file. Tap Shift, Ctrl or Alt on the way and drop the
file on a petal: the result is saved next to the original. There is no window to open, nothing to upload
and nothing to set up first.

Convert (Shift)
• Every format the file can become, with your starred favourites first: 342 conversion paths.
• Pictures: JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, ICO, GIF, SVG, PSD and camera RAW.
• Video and audio: MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG and more; video to GIF.
• Documents: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, Markdown, HTML, e-books and subtitles.

Tools (Ctrl)
• 53 tools: compress, resize and rotate, crop, watermark, redact, edit and annotate photos, collages,
  remove or replace a background, erase objects, upscale, copy text from a picture, QR codes,
  searchable PDFs, PDF passwords, page numbers and signatures, trim and split audio and video, and more.
• AI that stays on your PC: transcribe speech, read text aloud with natural voices, remove noise,
  separate vocals from music, translate English to Arabic.
• Ask Petal: say what you want, in English or Arabic ("make them 1080 px WEBP and zip them") and Petal
  builds the steps.

Actions (Alt)
• Share, send to your phone over Wi-Fi, clean files of hidden details before sharing, copy, move,
  rename, zip, open with another program, find duplicates, and your own folders to copy or move to.

Wheels without files (Ctrl + Win + Z with nothing selected)
• The app in front: ready-made shortcuts for browsers, File Explorer, VS Code, Word, Excel, PowerPoint,
  Teams, Zoom, Photoshop, Windows Terminal, Slack and Discord.
• Quick: dark mode, mute, microphone, keep awake, hidden files, file extensions, desktop icons,
  Bluetooth, lock, sleep, screenshots and more, each showing whether it is on.
• Media: play, pause, skip, shuffle and volume for whatever is playing, with its cover.
• Your own wheels and petals: keys to press, programs, PowerShell or Command Prompt scripts, folders,
  links, groups and the open windows.

Work faster
• A command palette (Ctrl+Win+K): type a few letters to run any action, open an app or a file, paste a
  clip or work out a sum.
• Live captions of any video, call or lesson, translated into Arabic as they come.
• Meeting notes: the transcript, a summary, the decisions and the action items, written on this PC.
• Snippets: ";addr" becomes your address in any app, with today's date or the Hijri date filled in.
• Workspaces that open your apps and put every window back, and focus sessions that keep distractions down.
• Screen recordings that zoom in where you click, with a smooth pointer and a clean background.
• Private screenshots with e-mails, phone and card numbers already covered.
• Send files to another PC on the same network, and share wheels as a line of text.

Folders work too
• Drop a folder to convert everything inside into a copy beside it, or organize it by kind or month,
  turn it into one PDF, a contact sheet or an Excel report.

Made for every day
• Recipes that chain steps, and watched folders that convert new files as they arrive.
• Find files by the words inside them, and photos by what they show.
• History that can put files back.
• Practice mode and a guided tour, number keys, the mouse wheel and a side button.
• Seven accent colours, glass, solid or monochrome wheels, animations, sounds and petal colours.
• Statistics of files handled and space saved, kept on this PC only.
• Back up everything to one file and carry it to another PC.
• A command line for terminals and scripts: petal convert, petal compress, petal ask…
• Fully in English and Arabic, right to left included.

Private by design
Everything runs on your PC. Petal has no account, no ads and no tracking, and never uploads your files.
AI models download once, the first time a tool needs them, from their authors' public pages.
